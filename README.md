## A little about me

I have a habit of becoming interested in the parts of technology that everyone else walks past.

The awkward handoff. The missing document. The model that sounds certain when it shouldn't. The workflow that takes twelve steps because nobody stopped to ask why there were twelve steps.

So I pull things apart.

Sometimes it's a system. Sometimes it's a process. Sometimes it's three models, six documents, a half-finished idea and one person who knows where everything is. Then I start putting it back together.

My work moves between software development, AI research, document intelligence, retrieval, automation and creative technology. I came to it through operations, public safety, compliance, events and music, which taught me to notice what breaks quietly and what people actually do when the procedure says something else.

I call myself an **AI Gardener** because that's mostly what the work feels like: plant something, watch it grow, cut out what isn't useful, and keep the parts that work.

## How I work

**Question → Research → Prototype → Test → Prune → Verify → Document**

I'm interested in the distance between something appearing to work and having enough evidence to trust it. That means examining sources, assumptions, failure cases, permissions and where a human needs to make the decision.

Not every problem needs another agent, framework or layer of automation. Sometimes the best improvement is removing something.
